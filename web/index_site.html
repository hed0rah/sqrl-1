databurn

SQRL-1 is free firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1, built on Felucca. It installs from the browser over USB, and the FM-1 can go back to its official firmware.

You play a pair: a SOUND, which makes the tone, and a NOTE engine, which decides the notes. Hold HOME and the octave from C4 to C5 becomes the wheel: a white key picks the SOUND, a black key the NOTE engine. The SOUNDs include six-operator FM, a drawn wave, a brass model, sung speech, a virtual analog, phase distortion, physical models and drum kits; the NOTE engines play chords, a beat of lanes, a line of lanes over it, a cellular automaton, the Tonnetz, a mutating loop and a strum plate.

Back to the official firmware

Download official V15, FM-1.fwsc, from M-VAVE's download page (FM-1, "PC Firmware") and use the installer's "Return to official V15": it checks the file, saves a complete backup, then installs it. M-VAVE's own updater, M-UPGRADE, does not see an FM-1 running SQRL-1; it works again once the FM-1 is back on V15. Details
